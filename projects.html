Our Past Projects and Initiatives

Over the years, TIDA Initiative has implemented programmes and campaigns in different locations, working to promote social inclusion, youth participation, democratic engagement, gender equality and community empowerment.

Our project history reflects our commitment to addressing social challenges, amplifying underrepresented voices and creating opportunities for individuals and communities to participate in positive social change. From youth violence prevention and democratic participation initiatives in West Africa to disability empowerment and community storytelling in the United Kingdom, these projects represent important milestones in our organisational journey.

We continue to build on the experience and lessons gained through these initiatives as we pursue our mission of creating inclusive, empowered and resilient communities.

Past and Completed Projects

The projects below are presented as historical initiatives. Their inclusion recognises work undertaken in previous years and does not imply that the activities are currently ongoing.

2018 | Nigeria

Say No to Youth Violence Campaign

The Say No to Youth Violence Campaign was a youth-focused initiative addressing violence among young people and promoting peace, positive engagement and peaceful coexistence within communities.

2019 | The Gambia and Nigeria

Civic Engagement and Elections Empowerment

This initiative took the form of a tour engaging young people in conversations about democracy, elections and their role in democratic processes. It aimed to encourage youth participation, civic awareness and active citizenship.

2020 | The Gambia

Women and Girls Empowerment Initiative

This initiative focused on the empowerment of women and girls, encouraging their participation, strengthening their voices and advancing opportunities for their development.

2023 | Manchester, United Kingdom

Empowerment for People with Disabilities

This initiative promoted the empowerment and inclusion of people with disabilities, with an emphasis on participation, visibility and recognition of their rights and contributions.
