COMING SOON

Learning Together. Growing Together. Achieving Together.

Together We Grow is a proposed community programme in Manchester designed to support children and young people aged 6–15 through learning, play, creativity and shared experiences.

Developed in response to the needs and ideas of local families, the programme aims to create welcoming opportunities for children to build confidence, develop skills, form friendships and reach their potential.

What the Programme Will Offer

Saturday Learning Support

Small-group English and Maths support to help children strengthen their learning, develop confidence and make progress.

Sunday Kids Club

Fun and engaging activities, including games, sports, arts, crafts, storytelling and opportunities to learn through play.

Monthly Outdoor Trips

Shared trips and experiences that help children explore new places, build friendships and develop a sense of belonging.

Our Vision

We want every child to feel included, supported and encouraged to achieve their potential, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Together We Grow is currently a proposed project awaiting a funding decision. Further information about its launch will be shared when arrangements are confirmed.
