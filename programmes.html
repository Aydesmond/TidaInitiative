Story Circles and Community Dialogues

We create safe, creative and welcoming spaces where people from migrant and diaspora communities can share their experiences, express themselves and connect with others.

Through storytelling, creative activities and community conversations, we bring people together to build understanding, strengthen relationships and make underrepresented experiences visible.

Our activities include:

  • Community storytelling circles
  • Creative writing, arts and photography
  • Intergenerational conversations
  • Community dialogues and discussion sessions
  • Story-sharing events and exhibitions

Community Voice, Power and Action

We support migrant and diaspora communities to understand their rights, raise their voices, organise collectively and influence the decisions and systems that affect their lives.

We believe communities should have an active role in shaping policies, services and decisions, rather than simply being consulted about them.

Our activities include:

  • Community advocacy and campaigning
  • Civic education and rights awareness
  • Youth voice and participation
  • Community organising and collective action
  • Engagement with decision-makers and institutions
  • Supporting communities to influence systemic change

Women’s Leadership and Empowerment

We support women from migrant and diaspora communities to build confidence, develop leadership skills and take active roles in their communities and wider society.

We create opportunities for women to connect, learn, share experiences and strengthen their influence.

Our activities include:

  • Leadership and personal development workshops
  • Confidence-building and public speaking
  • Peer support and mentoring
  • Skills development and networking
  • Opportunities for women to lead community initiatives
  • Supporting women’s participation in decision-making

Together for Inclusion and Development (TIDA)

Amplifying voices. Building collective power. Influencing systemic change.

Working with migrant and diaspora communities to advance inclusion, strengthen leadership and create lasting change.
